Instructions

Set the instructions that control how your AI bots behave.

Global Instructions

Used only when a bot does not have its own custom instructions. Bot-specific instructions replace the global instructions entirely.

Bot-specific Instructions

Select a bot and set its custom instructions. These replace the global instructions for that bot; they are not combined.

Changes are saved to Supabase first and are used by the Playground and external iframe after confirmation.
