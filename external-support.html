External Support Knowledge

Allow a bot to use real-time external support only after its own instructions, rules, and assigned knowledge do not provide the answer.

Global External Support

This master switch controls whether external support can be used at all. It does not change individual bot settings.

Bot External Support

Enable external support separately for each bot. A bot can use it only when both global and bot switches are ON and the normal knowledge sources do not answer the request.
